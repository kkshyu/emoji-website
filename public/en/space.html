1F

Emoji Café & Bar

Bright by day, moody by night. The 1F Emoji Café & Bar opens onto the street with a takeaway window, Japanese-style set meals and hand-brewed coffee — no membership required, a coffee is your ticket in.

After dark the lighting shifts and the counter turns into a late-night shokudō and bar: cocktails, snacks, and the occasional live set, open until 3am.

  • Japanese set meals & hand-brewed coffee
  • Late-night shokudō & bar
  • Long counter seating, occasional live music
  • Open to everyone — no membership needed

Café / Bar à la carte, NT$80–350 — coffee NT$80–160, lunch sets NT$180–220, drinks NT$150–350.

2F

Member Plaza — Members-only Rest

The Member Plaza is a members-only floor behind face-recognition entry — a place to rest, recharge and play between sessions.

Capsule rest pods with QR self check-in, private showers, and a play area with a Switch, board games and mahjong — the play room can also be booked out as a private party space.

  • Capsule rest pods (QR self check-in)
  • Private showers
  • Games & party room
  • 24-hour split access for members

Members only, redeemable with points — shower 7 pts/use, capsule & play room 10 pts/hour (Active membership required). Private play-room bookings: NT$10,000 per 2-hour session (cash), fully creditable toward same-day 1F food & drinks.

3F

Talent Lounge — Shared Coworking

The ceiling was stripped back to its original timber beams. Talent Lounge is a fully open, high-ceilinged floor with movable furniture — heads-down work by day, talks, Demo Nights and community events by night.

100-inch-plus projection, a self-serve snack bar, and a calendar full of matchmaking dinners, workshops and startup nights.

  • Open coworking desks
  • Event & workshop space
  • 100"+ projection screen
  • Self-serve snack bar

Day membership NT$200/4 hrs or NT$500/12 hrs; monthly NT$4,000, quarterly NT$10,000, annual NT$35,000 (with 100/300/1,000 gift points respectively, valid 1 year). Every membership includes free use of 3F. Private bookings: NT$20,000 per session (cash).
