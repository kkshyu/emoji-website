Partnership

Three ways to grow the network together

Scenarios come in every shape, but in practice they converge into three paths. Most collaborations start on one of them and adjust from there to fit your needs.

A

Event Salon · Host events for us

Be the curator and host: run recurring salons and events for the hub. We bring the venue, the community and the promotion; you bring the content — and together we build the hub's own event brand. A fit for speakers and curators who want a long-term stage.

B

Event Partner · Hold your events here

Move your classes, talks, book clubs or Demo Nights into the third-floor event space. From setup, flow, projection and the snack bar to check-in and ticketing, we run every event well together. A fit for instructors, hosts and teams who hold events regularly.

C

Event Circle · Join our alliance

Bring your community into the hub's alliance: co-host events, cross-refer, and share the hub's talent network and international connections. Your members become part of the hub's talent density, and the hub's network becomes an extension of your community.

Not sure which one you fit? That's completely normal. Most partners talk first, then find the right shape of collaboration together.

Scenarios

Six ways to bring people together

You don't need to figure out which kind of collaboration you want first. See which scenario feels like you, and we'll make it real together.

01 · Teachers with students

Move your class in

Teachers use the space for free, students pay for the class — no need to rent a separate classroom. You teach well; we get the venue, check-in and atmosphere ready.

02 · Talks & workshops

Get your course off the ground

Speakers who want to teach and share — from venue, projection and a snack bar to the audience, we help you turn a class from an idea into a full house.

03 · Hosts seeking a venue

Move your community in

Do your meetups, book clubs and gatherings need a stable, recurring venue? Move your community into the third floor, so it comes home to the same place every time.

04 · Teams running events

Get seen by the right people

Demo Nights, showcases, recruiting nights — put your work out there in a room full of founders, investors and talent, and let the right people see you.

05 · Company buyouts

The most inspiring venue nearest Taipei Main Station

Workshops, offsites, team co-creation or a full buyout — right next to Taipei Main Station, the easiest to reach, in a space full of inspiration.

06 · Cross-border communities

Plug into the international network

Digital nomads, overseas communities and cross-border teams connect through Emoji to digital-nomad and startup resources in Japan, Korea, Southeast Asia and beyond.

Why here

Why hosting here is different

For the same event, what matters isn't just how big the space is, but who shows up and what they can connect to.

Location

Next to Taipei Main Station

No. 11, Sec. 1, Chongqing S. Rd., Zhongzheng Dist., Taipei — diagonally across from Exit Z10 of the Taipei City Mall. A high-traffic node at Taiwan's largest station, the easiest place for your guests to reach from anywhere in the country.

Community

Built around talent density

This is where founders, freelancers, consultants, digital nomads and international talent gather. The people you bring in will meet a crowd they'd never usually meet — and that's exactly where collaboration is most valuable.

International

TDNA international network

Behind us are the Taiwan Digital Nomad Association (TDNA) and an international network reaching Japan, Korea, Southeast Asia and Europe, giving your events and community a chance to travel beyond Taiwan.

Space

Third-floor high-ceiling timber-beam event space

Movable furniture, multiple layouts, 100-inch-plus projection and a self-serve snack bar. From a book club of a dozen people to a packed Demo Night, it holds up.

The space

3F COMMONS: where talent density happens

Emoji is a three-story, roughly 265-square-meter physical hub next to Taipei Main Station. The stage for the Community Partner Program is mainly on the third floor.

3FCOMMONS

Community coworking + event space

High ceilings and timber beams, movable furniture and multiple layouts, paired with 100-inch-plus projection and a self-serve snack bar. It hosts talks, workshops, book clubs, meetups, Demo Nights, office hours, investor gatherings and full buyouts. Whether it's a deep discussion of a dozen people or a packed showcase, it holds up.

1FCAFÉ / BAR

Japanese-style café × late-night diner and bar

A buffer and after-party space before and after events. By day it's a working café; by night it switches into a late-night diner and Japanese-style bar — a lot of real collaborations get sealed at this table after an event wraps.

2FLOUNGE

Member capsule rest + game room

Speakers and guests coming from out of town can rest nearby, so a long trip doesn't mean rushing back the same day. (Members-only service, per the membership rules.)

The space is only infrastructure. What truly makes collaboration valuable is the people here and the network they weave.

Honest notes

A few things to be clear on before we collaborate

The Community Partner Program has no fixed packages; everything is discussed case by case. This page describes the spirit and possible shapes of collaboration — not the final terms.

Actual rights and obligations are governed by the agreement signed by both parties. That includes the scope and time slots of space use, fees or revenue share, how ticketing and lists are handled, and more — all spelled out one by one in the agreement.

The content on this page is a description of collaboration. We believe good collaboration is a relationship both sides can understand, account for clearly and find worthwhile.
