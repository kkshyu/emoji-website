Activities

Events & collaboration formats

Under our operating plan, the 3F event space mainly hosts the formats below. Outside collaborations are discussed case by case, with the actual arrangement set out in a written agreement.

01 · Talent Night

Weekly

Introductions, open mingling and host-led matchmaking. The hub's core community slot, where talent gets seen.

02 · Matchmaking dinners

2–4x a month

Cross-field tables of 6–8 people, where people from different backgrounds actually get talking over a meal.

03 · TDNA series

Per association calendar

This is TDNA's Taipei base, hosting the association's scheduled events and international-connection sessions.

04 · Talks & workshops

2–4x a month

Speakers are sourced from members first, with case-by-case collaboration open to outside instructors too.

05 · Tutoring & teaching

Free for teachers, paid for students

Teachers use the space free, students pay by the hour. No need to rent a separate classroom.

06 · Outside bookings

As needed

Workshops, offsites, team co-creation or full buyouts, scheduled and priced case by case.

TDNA Event Partner: venue-fee support is planned at 25–100% of the original plan; the actual share follows a written partnership agreement, approval process and supporting documentation.

The space

3F COMMONS: where talent density happens

Emoji is a three-story physical hub of about 281 m² next to Taipei Main Station. The main stage for Community Collaboration is on the third floor.

3FCOMMONS

Community coworking + event space

A 6-meter ceiling and timber beams, movable furniture and multiple layouts, paired with 100-inch-plus projection and a self-serve snack bar. It hosts talks, workshops, book clubs, meetups, Demo Nights, office hours, investor gatherings and full buyouts. Whether it's a deep discussion of a dozen people or a packed showcase, it holds up.

1FCAFÉ / BAR

Japanese-style café × late-night diner and bar

A buffer and after-party space before and after events. By day it's a working café; by night it switches into a late-night diner and Japanese-style bar. A lot of real collaborations get sealed at this table after an event wraps.

2FLOUNGE

24-hour reading & rest lounge + game & manga room

Speakers and guests coming from out of town can rest nearby, so a long trip doesn't mean rushing back the same day. (Members-only service, per the membership rules.)

The space is only infrastructure. What truly makes collaboration valuable is the people here and the network they weave.

Honest notes

A few things to be clear on before we collaborate

Community Collaboration has no fixed packages; everything is discussed case by case. This page describes the spirit and possible shapes of collaboration, not the final terms.

Actual rights and obligations are governed by the agreement signed by both parties. That includes the scope and time slots of space use, fees or revenue share, how ticketing and lists are handled, and more, all spelled out one by one in the agreement.

Space use must not crowd out our core community programming. We do not host direct-sales pitches, investment schemes, or religious or political mobilization events.

The content on this page is a description of collaboration. We believe good collaboration is a relationship both sides can understand, account for clearly and find worthwhile.
