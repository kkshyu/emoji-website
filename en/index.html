FAQ

Things you might want to know

What is Taiwan Talent Hub?

A building by Taipei Main Station designed to help you meet the right people. 1F is a Café & Bar, 2F is the Talent Member coworking space, 3F is Resident Member co-living, with year-round talks, events and startup matchmaking, connected to the international community of TDNA (Taiwan Digital Nomad Association). Come for a coffee, work here, or become a Resident and make the hub your home base.

Where is it?

No. 11, Section 1, Chongqing South Road, Zhongzheng District, Taipei — diagonally across from Exit Z10 of the Taipei Main Station underground mall, Taiwan's busiest transit node.

When does it open?

The soft opening is planned for September 1, 2026, open daily 11:00–03:00. The grand-opening date will be announced after the soft-opening period. Subscribe to the newsletter to be the first to hear about opening news, progress updates and limited Fellow spots.

What are the plans and prices?

Day Pass NT$350, 10-visit pass NT$2,800; Flex membership NT$3,500/month (12 days a month), Full-time membership NT$5,000/month; team rooms / private offices NT$18,000–22,000/month; Resident Member co-living NT$20,000–24,000/month. Café & Bar items NT$80–350; event bookings from NT$20,000.

Who is it for?

Digital nomads, remote workers, freelancers, engineers, designers, product managers, founders, international talent and investors — anyone who wants a home base in Taipei, a wider network, and opportunities to collaborate or start something new.

What is a Fellow?

Fellows are a pre-opening-only circle — the first people to back the hub and build its culture together. Spots are limited and open in order of joining; see fellow.taiwantalenthub.com for full details.
